This Privacy Policy explains how Reducto, Inc. (“Reducto,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and retains personal information in connection with Parse Judge, a free website at parsejudge.com that runs the document parsers you choose on documents you upload, has an AI model grade their outputs, and reports the results (the “Service”). Reducto also offers its own document parser, which you can select in the Service (the “Reducto Parser”). This Privacy Policy applies only to Parse Judge. It works together with the Parse Judge Terms of Service at <https://parsejudge.com/terms> (the “Terms”), and capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Terms. Our other products are covered by separate notices, including our general Privacy Notice at reducto.ai/privacy.

## 1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information. Your email address, the time you created your account, and your run-email preference. To protect sign-in from abuse, we also keep your email address and IP address with each sign-in attempt for about 24 hours.

1.2 Content. Documents you upload and their file names, the run names and settings you choose, the instructions and tool calls an AI assistant or agent sends to the Service on your behalf, and the Parser outputs, scores, and reports the Service creates (“Content”). Content may include personal information about you or others.

1.3 API Keys. If you provide your own API key for a Parser, we encrypt it, use it only for that run, and delete it when the run finishes, fails, is stopped, or is deleted. An encrypted copy may remain in our database restore history for up to 30 days.

1.4 Platform Information. If you connect the Service to an AI assistant or agent (each, a “Platform”), we receive only what the Platform sends to perform a requested action, such as the request, relevant files, API keys for Parsers that require your own key, and technical identifiers. We do not receive your full conversation history unless the Platform includes it in a request.

1.5 Usage and Device Information. Log data, IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, browser and device type, timestamps, features used, request volume, errors, and similar information, including our hosting provider’s operational logs and traces. We design them to exclude the contents of your documents, Parser outputs, and the AI model’s grading.

1.6 Cookies and Similar Technologies. We use two cookies, both to keep you signed in and to protect sign-in from abuse. Your browser’s local storage also holds an upload you start before signing in and your choice of Parsers to compare. On the sign-in page, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are not a bot. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies or tools. We do not allow third parties to collect information about your online activities over time and across third-party websites through the Service. We do not respond to browser Do Not Track signals because we do not track users across third-party websites.

1.7 Communications. Information you send when you contact support or give feedback.

## 2. How We Use Information

2.1 Purposes. We use personal information to: (a) provide, operate, and secure the Service, including processing Content to perform requested actions; (b) manage accounts and Rate Limits; (c) provide support and communicate with you, including emailing you when a run finishes; (d) analyze aggregated usage to improve the Service; (e) detect and prevent fraud and abuse; and (f) comply with law and enforce our Terms.

2.2 No Training. We do not use Content to train or improve AI models, including the Reducto Parser.

## 3. How We Disclose Information

3.1 Service Providers. We use vendors that perform services on our behalf, such as cloud hosting, email delivery, abuse prevention, and AI model processing. They may use personal information only to provide services to us.

3.2 Parsers. When you start a run, we send your documents to the Parsers you select, and we send pages and Parser outputs to AI model providers that grade and summarize them, and we send the file names of your documents to an AI model provider to name the run. If you select the Reducto Parser, Reducto processes your documents under this Privacy Policy. For a third-party Parser, the Service uses either Reducto’s account with that provider or your own API key. Under Reducto’s account, the provider processes documents under its agreement with Reducto. With your own API key, your agreement with that provider governs its processing. Third-party Parsers keep documents under their own terms, which may allow them to keep documents after your run.

3.3 Shared Reports. If you share a report by link, anyone with the link can view it. You can stop sharing or create a new link at any time.

3.4 Platforms. We return outputs to the Platform you use to fulfil your request. The Platform’s handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy.

3.5 Legal and Safety. To comply with law or legal process, protect rights and safety, or enforce our Terms.

3.6 Corporate Transactions. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

3.7 No Sale. We do not sell or license personal information or Content, or share it for advertising purposes.

## 4. Retention

4.1 Default Retention. We retain account information and Content by default while your account is active, so you can return to your runs.

4.2 After Account Closure. We treat account closure as a deletion request under Section 4.3.

4.3 Deletion. You can delete a run’s documents, a run, or all of your Content at any time in the Service, or request deletion at [**support@reducto.ai**](mailto:support@reducto.ai). We delete Content from active systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days, unless law requires us to keep it. Deletion applies to copies Reducto holds. It does not reach copies that a third-party Parser or AI model provider received to complete your run. If you delete only a run’s documents, we keep the run’s title, which is generated from your file names, and its summary until you delete the run.

4.4 Logs. We keep operational logs for 7 days and usage metrics, which do not include Content, for 3 months. We keep sign-in records for about 24 hours.

## 5. Your Choices and Rights

5.1 Account and Content. You can delete your Content and close your account in Settings. To access, update, or export your information, or to close your account, email [**support@reducto.ai**](mailto:support@reducto.ai).

5.2 Emails. You can turn off run emails on your account page.
