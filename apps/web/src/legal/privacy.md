This Privacy Policy explains how Reducto, Inc. ("Reducto," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and retains personal information in connection with Parse Judge, a free website at parsejudge.com that runs the document parsers you choose on documents you upload, has an AI model grade their outputs, and reports the results (the "Service"). It applies only to Parse Judge. This Privacy Policy works together with the Parse Judge Terms of Service at [https://parsejudge.com/terms](https://parsejudge.com/terms) (the "Terms"), and capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Terms. Our other products are covered by separate notices, including our general Privacy Notice at [reducto.ai/privacy](https://reducto.ai/privacy).

## 1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information. When you sign in: your email address and authentication identifiers.

1.2 Content. Documents you upload, the run names and settings you choose, the instructions and tool calls an AI assistant or agent sends to the Service on your behalf, and the Parser outputs, scores, and reports the Service creates ("Content"). Content may include personal information about you or others.

1.3 API Keys. If you provide your own API key for a Parser, we encrypt it, use it only for that run, and delete it when the run ends.

1.4 Platform Information. If you connect the Service to an AI assistant or agent (each, a "Platform"), we receive only what the Platform sends to perform a requested action, such as the request, relevant files, and technical identifiers. We do not receive your full conversation history unless the Platform includes it in a request.

1.5 Usage and Device Information. Log data, IP address, browser and device type, timestamps, features used, request volume, errors, and similar information.

1.6 Cookies. We use cookies to keep you signed in and to protect sign-in from abuse. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

1.7 Communications. Information you send when you contact support or give feedback.

## 2. How We Use Information

2.1 Purposes. We use personal information to: (a) provide, operate, and secure the Service, including processing Content to perform requested actions; (b) manage accounts and Rate Limits; (c) provide support and communicate with you, including emailing you when a run finishes; (d) analyze aggregated usage to improve the Service; (e) detect and prevent fraud and abuse; and (f) comply with law and enforce our Terms.

2.2 No Training. We do not use Content to train or improve AI models.

## 3. How We Disclose Information

3.1 Service Providers. We use vendors that perform services on our behalf, such as cloud hosting, email delivery, abuse prevention, and AI model processing. They may use personal information only to provide services to us.

3.2 Parsers. When you start a run, we send your documents to the document parsing providers you select (each, a "Parser"), and we send pages and Parser outputs to AI model providers that grade and summarize them. Each Parser processes documents under its own terms. If you use your own API key, your agreement with that Parser governs its processing.

3.3 Shared Reports. If you share a report by link, anyone with the link can view it. You can stop sharing or create a new link at any time.

3.4 Platforms. We return outputs to the Platform you use to fulfil your request. The Platform's handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy.

3.5 Legal and Safety. To comply with law or legal process, protect rights and safety, or enforce our Terms.

3.6 Corporate Transactions. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

3.7 No Sale. We do not sell or license personal information or Content, or share it for advertising purposes.

## 4. Retention

4.1 Default Retention. We retain account information and Content by default while your account is active, so you can return to your runs.

4.2 After Account Closure. We treat account closure as a deletion request under Section 4.3.

4.3 Deletion. You can delete a run's documents, a run, or all of your Content at any time in the Service, or request deletion at [support@reducto.ai](mailto:support@reducto.ai). We delete Content from active systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days, unless law requires us to keep it.

## 5. Your Choices and Rights

5.1 Account and Content. You can delete your Content in the Service. To access, update, export, or close your account, email [support@reducto.ai](mailto:support@reducto.ai).

5.2 Emails. You can turn off run emails on your account page.

You may use an authorized agent for opt-out or deletion where the state explicitly grants you the right to use an authorized agent as such. If we deny your request, you may appeal by emailing [support@reducto.ai](mailto:support@reducto.ai) with "Appeal" in the subject line.
